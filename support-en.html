Frequently asked questions

How many free discoveries do I get?

The free version includes 3 new discoveries a day (the first guided discovery doesn’t count). The counter resets at midnight, iPhone time. Species you have already discovered always remain available.

What does Premium unlock?

Unlimited discoveries, every species in the catalogue, every collection, country, continent and expert quizzes, and species comparisons. Premium is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore my purchases?

In the app: Profile › Premium › Restore purchases (the button is also at the bottom of the Premium screen). Use the same Apple Account you purchased with.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app: Profile › Premium › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

What happens to my species if my subscription ends?

They stay in your collection. Only Premium features (unlimited discoveries, undiscovered Premium content…) become unavailable.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone: collection, favourites, quizzes, badges and preferences. There is no account and no online sync: deleting the app erases this data.

How do I change the language?

Profile › Language: English, French or system language. The change is instant.

Does the app work offline?

Yes for your collection, field guides, photos and quizzes. The map needs a connection; offline, the Explore tab shows a list view.

Where do the photos and facts come from?

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, with the author credited on each field guide (Profile › Sources and credits). Conservation statuses refer to the IUCN Red List. Spotted a mistake? Let us know.
