Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

No account required

The Living Atlas works without signing up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Data kept on your device

Your collection, favourites, quiz results, badges and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not sent to our servers. Deleting the app erases this data.

No tracking, no ads

The app contains no ads, no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics kits. We do not sell or share any data.

Location

The app does not access your location. The map shows the world without locating you.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store which content you can access.

Maps and notifications

The map uses Apple Maps, subject to Apple’s privacy policy. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, only if you turn them on.

Children

The app collects no personal data, including from children.

Contact

For any privacy question, please use the Support page.
