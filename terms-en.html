Premium subscriptions

Premium is offered as an auto-renewable monthly (1 month) or yearly (1 year) subscription, and as a one-time lifetime purchase. The applicable price is shown in the app before purchase, in your local currency.

Payment and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically at the same price and duration unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.

Managing and cancelling

Manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the app (Profile › Premium › Manage subscription) or in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid.

After your subscription

Species you have already discovered stay in your collection even if your subscription ends.
